## Uniswap DEX, Polymarket, Stablecoin and Cross Border Payment Market

In Week 5, I went through the differences in Uniswap v1-v4 on a high level, and also the DEX lecture from Berkeley MOOC. Week 6 covered Polymarket transaction examples for intuition, and I learned more about stablecoins, the why, the Genius Act, and cross-border transfers.

For international cash transfers in the traditional banking infrastructure, some examples of settlement processes look like:
1. Swift message system + CHIPS (netting system, finally settled on Fedwire)
2. Swift message system + Fedwire
3. Swift GPI

While the first two take 1-3 business days to complete the settlement process, Swift GPI is instantaneous, but adoption is still in progress, possibly with higher processing fees than [stablecoin gas fees](https://www.bvnk.com/blog/blockchain-cross-border-payments). There is a [220B cross-border payment market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/cross-border-payments-market?utm_source=chatgpt.com) in 2025. With the Genius ACT under discussion, I am very excited to observe the global payment system taking shape with blockchain being part of it.
