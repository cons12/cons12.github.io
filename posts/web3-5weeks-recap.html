Week6: Recap on 5 weeks of learning web3

May 25, 2025
Web3

This is a recap of my 5-week Web3 learning plan.

Solana Hands-on Dev and Solana Accelerate in NYC

In Week 3, I didn’t finish the Solana tutorial bootcamp YouTube videos. This was partly because the tutorial was from 2022, and the Solana toolchains in the video were not up to date, as well as the Rust versions, which created many hiccups while learning new concepts. On the other hand, I don’t know much about Rust, so if I have any plans in the future for getting more into Solana development, I will learn Rust first.

By coincidence, Solana Accelerate was happening in NYC in May, so Week 4 turned into attending the in-person workshop at Solana Labs NYC. I talked to some people, gained hands-on experience with on-chain development, and discovered a good Romanian knish place.

A new version of the tutorial that I could go through in the future if I have some interest is: https://github.com/solana-developers/developer-bootcamp-2024, with fuller examples.

Uniswap DEX, Polymarket, Stablecoin and Cross Border Payment Market

In Week 5, I went through the differences in Uniswap v1-v4 on a high level, and also the DEX lecture from Berkeley MOOC. Week 6 covered Polymarket transaction examples for intuition, and I learned more about stablecoins, the why, the Genius Act, and cross-border transfers.

For international cash transfers in the traditional banking infrastructure, some examples of settlement processes look like:

  1. Swift message system + CHIPS (netting system, finally settled on Fedwire)
  2. Swift message system + Fedwire
  3. Swift GPI

While the first two take 1-3 business days to complete the settlement process, Swift GPI is instantaneous, but adoption is still in progress, possibly with higher processing fees than stablecoin gas fees. There is a 220B cross-border payment market in 2025. With the Genius ACT under discussion, I am very excited to observe the global payment system taking shape with blockchain being part of it.

Afterthoughts

The initial plan-making was indeed a successful one, I executed nearly 100% of it, and discovered Accelerate Bootcamp and the quite but radical revolution of stablecoin along the way. I am now equipped with more knowledge in this Web3 field than before, and I know what I am talking about when using concepts and can explain them to my friends. I will keep learning, but I am shifting gears toward DDIA for now because I am so curious about it, and because a friend gifted me a paperback of it:)
